🏁 Race — tracks: Circuit, City, Snow, Rainbow and ✈️ Airport (taxiways past the runway, planes and the control tower). 1–5 laps. Beat the AI and your 👻 ghost lap.

💥 Derby — bump every car off the arena into the lava. Last car standing wins. The arena shrinks after 30s. MEGA = 20 cars, Obstacles = bumpers, spinning bar & trapdoor holes.

⚽ Soccer — 2v2, 3v3 or 4v4. Score in the other goal, 2 minutes, tie = golden goal. Press C for ball cam.

🛹 Stunt — 2 minutes in a skatepark. Points for air time and spins, +250 for flying through rings.

👑 King — 5v5. Stay in the glowing zone: 5 🪙 per car every 15s. More cars inside than the enemy = you hold it. First to 100 wins.

💣 Potato — 8 cars, one has a ticking bomb. Bump a car to pass it! When the timer hits 0 it explodes and that car is out. Fuse gets shorter each round. Last car left wins.

📦 Delivery — City. Drive into the yellow beam to grab a package, then the green beam to deliver it. Each delivery adds time. How many can you do?

🚩 CTF — 4v4 Capture the Flag. Steal their flag and bring it to your base (your flag must be home). Hit an enemy carrier to make them drop it. First to 3.

🚦 Red Light — 8 cars on a long straight. 🟢 green = GO, 🔴 red = FREEZE! Still moving 1.3s after red turns on? You're caught and out. First across the line wins.

🎵 Chairs — Musical Chairs with parking spots! Drive around while the music plays. When it stops, park in a 🅿️ spot (one less spot than cars). No spot = out. Shove others out of theirs! Last car wins.

🚓 Chase — outrun the cops on the desert highway. Each hit = 5% damage, 100% = BUSTED. Reach the finish to escape.

Fun modes (mix them!): 🔥 Lava — off the road and you burn · 🦖 Giant — huge cars, big bumps · 💥 Damage — crashes slow you down, 100% = wrecked · 🌙 Night — headlights in the dark · 🦘 Jumps — ramps on the race tracks

🚗 / 🏍️ Vehicle — the Bike speeds up faster and turns tighter, but gets knocked around more. (No bikes in the Derby: everyone drives a car there.)

⭐ Levels & XP — every match gives XP (+50, +100 for a win, plus bonuses). Level up to unlock vehicles: 🚀 Rocket (Lv 3: fastest, turns wider), 🚓 Police Car (Lv 5: heavy, wins bumps), 🏆 Gold Car (Lv 8: quick all-rounder).

👻 Ghost power — press G (P2: \) to drive through other cars for 3 seconds. Recharges in 15s.

🌈 Rainbow — a neon track in space: fall off and you respawn.

🚀 Perfect Start — press gas in the last moment before GO (not too early!) for a launch boost.

🎨 Paint Shop — the button on the right: color every part, add ✨ glow and 🔥 decals.

👥 2 Players — name your players, pick Versus or Co-op, with or without AI.

📊 Stats — your wins, KOs, goals, top speed and 🏆 achievements.

🎬 Replay — after every match: TV cams, slow-mo, follow any car.
